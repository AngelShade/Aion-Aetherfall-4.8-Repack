# Aion Aetherfall 4.8 Repack — Changelog

## Next update

- Beshmundir and Gelkmaros repeat quests now pay bonus supplies, and the Warped Rift event pays potions for your level.
- Rift Stones and alliance General hunts now pay medals, and General hunts have a small chance of armour for your class.
- The Swimsuit Swap event quest now gives you the swimsuit.
- Male and female companions now have their own portraits for every class, on recruit results, cards and profiles.
- Free bots passing your fight now join in against elite monsters, or when your health is low. The kill and its rewards stay yours.
- Bots now travel across their map to hunt monsters that drop the gear they want.
- Bots no longer dive down elevator shafts or sink through town floors when they get stuck.
- Falling a long way now ends when you hit the floor, instead of partway down.
- Road Wardens who belong to a player's legion now stay on patrol.
- Large companion parties keep their line formation close, and companions can equip stat-bearing remodel armor they own or earn.
- The companion equipment window lets you inspect equipped Stigmas and other items outside the paperdoll.
- Road Wardens can still offer rescue after you reconnect while dead from a monster attack.
- Bots no longer pile up on rock ledges. A stuck bot leaps down, or moves to a new hunting spot if that fails.
- New bots start out against monsters they can beat, instead of dying and reappearing over and over in starter zones.
- Bots hunt elite monsters for the gear they want, and ask for help when one is too strong for them.
- Bots buy gear on the Central Market with budgets that match their level.
- Bots keep fighting instead of healing themselves too early, and no longer stand around for hours on empty maps.
- Summon now works while your Find Group party waits at a dungeon, brings everyone who can come, and explains who couldn't.
- Targeting yourself or a monster no longer blocks Summon.
- Dungeon parties from Find Group keep filling after you join, and late arrivals wait with the group instead of teleporting to you.
- Faces catch the light with more natural depth around the eyes, nose and lips.
- You can now move the Spirit Bar with Move UI, and it no longer hides its Pet Auto-attack button behind your first action bar.
- Monsters no longer gain Razorlight Veil and other self-buffs from your skills.
- Exploding monsters no longer hit you with their own self-destruct damage.

Characters look better than ever up close, with repainted faces and silky, glowing skin.

### New

- See a bot's gear rarity and combat rank beside the target and party windows, and in the Find Group hire question.
- World bots now have real trades. They gather and craft even on maps with no player, using real materials and their own Kinah.
- Bots trade through the Central Market: gatherers keep it stocked, and crafters post buy orders you can sell into.
- Bots differ in how they trade. Some sell little and often, while hoarders build up big stocks and sell in large lots.
- Open Help requests from Find Group to review companions' requests, keep accepted objectives after reconnecting, and track, locate, abandon or collect rewards.
- Invite a Road Warden for a free escort toward either end of the road, lasting up to 15 minutes.
- Take a daily Road Bounty from a Warden for XP and Kinah. Every third completed bounty earns a Recruitment Ticket.
- Nearby Road Wardens answer monster distress, offer safe roadside resurrection and share a protective blessing.

### Improved

- Bots now use traps, totems, servants, summoned areas, dashes and stealth in real fights, and their Stigma builds include these skills.
- Game updates and other mods are less likely to stop Aion launching when you use the Vulkan option or cursor fix.
- Fire Temple, Nochsana, Beshmundir, Empyrean Crucible and 15 more dungeons can now pay Recruitment Tickets and teach your companions when you finish them.
- Road Wardens walk longer roads outside settlements, wear regional company names and titles, and report nearby threats when selected.
- Road Wardens spread out along roads. Extra Wardens return to ordinary field activity until a patrol place opens.
- Warden help leaves your loot intact. Earn full XP and bounty credit when your team deals at least a fifth of the combined damage.
- World companions now hunt monsters that drop the gear they want, and companions after the same item split up.
- Help requests now use a tidy board you can filter and page through, showing each request's map and level range.
- The Party quest tracker now shows where to go for every quest, with a button that marks it on your map.
- Repainted base player faces and bare bodies in much higher resolution, with sharper eyes, silky skin and a soft sheen. Face variations are sharper too.

### Fixed

- The Nether Dragon King's Sealed Box from the Season Pass lets you choose your weapon or shield again, as it promises.
- World bots fill the world properly again. A bot with nowhere to land could hold all the others up.
- A Map Made of Stone now gives you its random crafting design.
- Pollution Solution now gives you its crafting materials, and fortress quests pay their bonus medals.
- You have the intended time to use a chain skill after the previous skill hits.
- You leave the world as soon as quitting finishes; losing your connection keeps your character in the world for ten seconds.
- You can finish affected campaign quests without missing conversations or stalled steps.
- You land reliably after flight transports, including Ascension and early campaign flights.
- You can progress through Empyrean Crucible's revised stages, bosses and bonus rounds with your companions.
- You take corrected fall damage, return to your bind point immediately after a lethal fall, and avoid fall damage from lifts.
- Server hosts can still adjust fall damage amounts and heights in the server settings.
- You can complete escort quests when the follower reaches its destination.
- You see other players start, stop, glide and move on platforms correctly.
- You get five full minutes to duel after the countdown, and teleporting ends your duel.

- Fixed warehouse deposits stopping before all slots were filled and items disappearing when you dragged them.
- Fixed Monodia in Henor Village so you can take the quest Desperation from her instead of getting a load error.
- Fixed travelling merchant shops so you receive purchased items and see a reason when a purchase is refused.
- Fixed companion help requests causing errors when world companions arrive, and reduced pauses during companion decisions, companion trading and Central Market updates.
- Fixed long server freezes when too many world companions filled the game server's memory.
- Fixed world companions staying idle without a destination and getting stranded in areas unsuitable for their level.
- Fixed world companions skipping their equipment goal refresh.
- Fixed Show location doing nothing on Help requests.
- Fixed the Party quest tracker showing no turn-in location when the NPC is on another map.
- Fixed the game server taking minutes to stop after using Find Group invitations, which made the Hub's Stop buttons look stuck.
- Fixed Central Market errors appearing in the server log when the game server stops.

- Fixed a "load fail" message when you confirm a quest reward again at an NPC offering a follow-up quest.
- Fixed companions drawing and sheathing their weapon over and over between buffs and heals.
- Fixed dotted, mesh-like patches on trees, hanging roots, arches and glowing plants, and overly bright lamp glows, in zones with sharper textures.

Build your Companions collection, watch each new recruit's reveal, and choose who joins your party. Road Wardens, regional merchants and new Journey chapters give you more to discover as you play.

Your characters, progress and settings carry over.

### New
- **Companions collection:** browse illustrated companion cards, filter and sort your collection, and open an Overview to see each companion's details.

- **50 starter tickets:** your account receives them once when you first open the ticket wallet, including through Companions. Existing accounts receive them too.
- **Recruitment reveal:** see your new companion's appearance in an animated reveal. Use Continue or Skip to move through the results.
- **Companion recycling:** recycle a Ticket Recruit for Ticket credit, Learning XP for another chosen Ticket Recruit, and Kinah. Review the rewards before confirming.
- **Hire world companions:** check a companion's hiring price and confirm the cost before inviting them into your party.
- **Gear plans:** save equipment presets and see which pieces your companion still needs.
- **Stigma builds:** choose from four builds for each advanced companion class.
- **Collection rewards:** grow your collection's Renown, earn Kinah stipends, and follow companion progress through Chronicle and Season Pass goals.
- **Share gear:** transfer earned or gifted equipment between active Ticket Recruits in your collection.
- **Road Wardens:** meet companions who patrol roads and fight nearby monsters. Invite one to escort you along the road.
- **Regional merchants:** browse changing regional stock. Merchants in Oriel and Pernon also sell housing goods.
- **Companion quest goals:** follow companion-help objectives in your quest journal and tracker, with more room for active quests.
- **Journey chapters:** follow six chapters covering recruitment, companion growth, party help and fighting together, with titles to earn along the way.

### Improved
- **Companions look after their rewards:** while idle, they claim Pass rewards and stipends, collect Daeva Pass reward mail, and open reward boxes.
- **Party orders:** companions prioritize your orders and combat. Recruited road companions pause their patrol duties while helping you.
- **Companion windows:** read clearer Benefits, mission, reward and party-quest pages, with collection actions available directly on companion cards.
- **Find Group:** use clearer companion listings, party vacancies and party-filling offers to find help for your group.
- **Journey:** enjoy updated artwork and reopen the continuation page whenever you want to pick it up again.
- **Loot windows:** move the loot-roll rows together. Rows close gaps after rolls finish and wrap to fit shorter screens.

### Fixed
- **Recruitment artwork:** fixed the red “Replace Me” screen during companion recruitment.
- **Warden patrols:** road Wardens now choose roads that have monsters to fight.
- **Journey connections:** fixed opening Journey when you connect to a server on another computer.
- **Quest conversations:** corrected F, 1 and Enter controls when speaking with NPCs.
- **Pollution Resolution:** repaired Lepios's quest conversation and moved a trapped Cantas Coast Sea Starturtle out of collision.
- **Warehouse:** corrected right-click item transfers.

### For server hosts
- **Temporary Bots:** free recruitment is off by default, including saved Temporary Bots. Ticket recruitment and recruiting your account characters are available.
- **Enable free recruitment:** in `Server/game-server/config/playerbots/playerbots.properties`, set `gameserver.playerbots.generated.enable = true`, then restart GameServer.
- **Turn it off again:** set `gameserver.playerbots.generated.enable = false`. This also blocks recruiting missing Temporary Bots from saved parties.
- **Starter grant:** in `Server/game-server/config/playerbots/features.properties`, `gameserver.playerbots.tickets.starting = 50` controls the once-per-account grant. Set `0` to disable future grants.
- **Existing ticket balances:** starter tickets add to your balance once. Changing the grant amount or relogging does not award them again.
- **Shared server address:** in `Server/server-address.properties`, set `server.address` to the IP address or hostname your players connect to, then restart the servers.
