AETHERFALL RELEASE — UPLOAD, SEED & UPDATE GUIDE

CURRENT PREPARED RELEASES
Use Prepare Patch, then Publish Update from the release workspace. This also
prepares/uploads a clean current Aetherfall-Downloader.zip and updates the public
README download links. Share the stable link after publication:
https://github.com/AngelShade/Aion-Aetherfall-4.8-Repack/releases/latest/download/Aetherfall-Downloader.zip
Do not send new users the historical v0.86 downloader ZIP. The large v0.86 base
archive remains compatible. Users already downloading can replace only Setup
while it is closed, keeping their settings and Downloads folder to resume.

INITIAL BASE ARCHIVE / MANUAL MIRROR PUBLICATION
1. Upload "Aetherfall-0.86.7z" and "Aetherfall-Downloader.zip" to your Google Drive
   and MEGA folders.
2. Share "Aetherfall-Downloader.zip" (plus your public Google Drive / MEGA links,
   "Aetherfall-0.86.torrent", and "magnet.txt") with players.
   Players only need to open "Aetherfall Setup.exe" inside Aetherfall-Downloader.zip.
3. To seed "Aetherfall-0.86.7z" over BitTorrent from your PC, run "Start-Seeding.cmd"
   (or add Aetherfall-0.86.torrent to your torrent client pointed at this folder).
4. Optional Mirror Links:
   - Edit "Aetherfall-Downloader\mirrors.json":
     * Put Google Drive / MEGA public share links in "SharePages".
     * Put direct raw HTTPS download URLs (if any) under "Files" -> "Aetherfall-0.86.7z".
   - Then run:
     python publish.py "<DISTRIBUTION_ROOT>"
     to refresh Aetherfall-Downloader.zip.
5. Creating Future Small Updates (0.87+):
   - After updating your repack folder, run:
     python build_patch.py --package "<RepackPath>" --baseline "baseline-files-0.86.json" --output "updates"
   - Share the small "Aetherfall-Patch-<version>.zip" (or host "latest-update.json").
   - Players just click "Update Repack" inside Aetherfall Setup.exe / Aetherfall Hub.exe.
