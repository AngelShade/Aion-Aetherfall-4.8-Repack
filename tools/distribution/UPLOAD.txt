AETHERFALL RELEASE - UPLOAD AND SEED

1. Upload EVERY .7z.NNN file from the Aetherfall-<version>-parts folder, preserving
   names. Do not change, recompress or rename the individual parts.
2. Share Aetherfall-Downloader.zip, the .torrent file, SHA256SUMS.txt and magnet.txt.
3. Keep Start-Seeding.cmd running on a machine holding the complete parts folder.
   Default owner upload limit is 2 MiB/s; edit it if desired. Seeding verifies
   existing data first and then stays online until you close it. Ports and NAT
   affect inbound peers; this package does not alter firewall/router settings.
4. Google Drive and MEGA require PUBLIC share links after upload. Account home
   and /fm/ links do not identify public downloads. Their browser pages are
   manual backup mirrors; do not paste them as direct HTTP/webseed URLs.
5. For a true torrent + HTTP hybrid, host this exact directory structure:
      https://host/base/Aetherfall-<version>-parts/Aetherfall-<version>.7z.001
   Then put https://host/base/ in mirrors.json WebSeeds. Range requests must work.
6. For unrelated direct per-part URLs, fill mirrors.json Files with each exact
   archive name mapped to an array of URLs. HTTP mode uses all supplied mirrors.
7. Publish mirrors.json at a stable public HTTPS URL. Set MirrorsUrl in the
   downloader's settings.json and rezip the small downloader once. Future URL
   edits then need no new downloader ZIP, provided content is unchanged.
8. Use publish.py after editing mirrors.json to emit a Metalink, add webseeds
   outside the torrent's info dictionary, update its checksum and rebuild the
   downloader ZIP. It preserves the torrent swarm info hash and archive hashes.

No hosting accounts are configured, no files are uploaded, and no seeding daemon
is started by the packer. Creating a torrent does not create an online seed.
