# Aetherfall launcher branding

These are unchanged copies of the maintained Aetherfall launcher assets:

- wordmark.png: client-mods/launcher/assets/aetherfall-logo.png
- launcher-banner.png: client-mods/launcher/assets/atreia.png
- frame.png: client-mods/launcher/assets/frame.png
- crest.ico: client-mods/launcher/assets/aetherfall-hub.ico

The inline SVG wordmark uses the exact launcher crop: viewBox 0 146 2048 460,
from a 2048 by 768 PNG. No text was regenerated or painted over the logo.
The full image and transparency are preserved. See Launcher.cs and SetupApp.cs
for the matching CroppedBitmap rectangle. Website hero art and real gameplay
screenshots remain separate from the launcher banner.
