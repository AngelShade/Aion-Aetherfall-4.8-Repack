# Aetherfall website artwork

`hero-v1.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this
website. It is promotional illustration, not a gameplay screenshot.

Prompt: A finished cinematic Aetherfall hero. Atreia-inspired floating continents,
ancient ivory fortress and broken stone terraces above deep clouds, small cyan
aether crystals and a restrained gold horizon. Six adventurers including a shield
bearer, archer, healer and winged Daeva stand on the right facing the world. Wide
16:9 painted game key art. Left 45 percent dark and calm for live HTML copy. Deep
navy, ice silver, pale cyan and muted antique gold. Grounded fantasy armor and
natural anatomy. No words, logos, UI or watermarks.

The four JPGs in `screenshots/` are actual client captures provided by the owner.
Their pixels are unchanged; embedded game character, equipment and location metadata is removed from the public copies. The website crops them through a CSS viewport using
coordinates in `config/aetherfall_content.php`, and the gallery links to each full
image. They show a companion party in Inggison, questing in Tolbas Forest,
Taloc's Hollow and Eltnen Fortress. Original screenshot filenames and hashes are
kept in the local delivery record, not public player pages.
