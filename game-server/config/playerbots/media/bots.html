Two ways to build your party

TEMPORARY BOT

Follows your level with a managed class build. Keeps its own earned progress and equipment for future recruitment.

ACCOUNT CHARACTER

A real level-one character, or recruit an existing alt. Existing alts keep their equipment, skills and Stigmas.

YOUR PARTY, SAVED

Mix both types. Save the party once, then recruit it again from your roster.

Temporary Bots receive class and role equipment, skills and Stigmas. Gear tiers advance at levels 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60. Dismissal saves their characters; Save Temporary Bot checkpoints progress. Player-owned alts retain their existing builds.

A full ordinary party can expand into a native alliance when your configured active limit permits it. Alliances hold up to 24 total members. Native level, faction and team rules apply; an active combat timer prevents recruitment.
