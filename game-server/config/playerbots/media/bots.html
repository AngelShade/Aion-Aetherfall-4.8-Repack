A companion that grows with you

Each companion keeps their own equipment, quests and progression.

Temporary Bots follow your level, receive class and role equipment, learn skills and configure Stigmas. Gear tiers advance at levels 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60. They leave the world when dismissed; your private roster keeps them for later recruitment. Use Save Temporary Bot to checkpoint progress, then find them in Saved Temporary Bots. Save a mixed party from the Party tab to summon it again. Player-owned alts keep their equipment, builds and Stigmas.

Recruitment follows the normal level, faction and party rules. An active combat timer prevents recruitment.
