AION AETHERFALL
REPACK NEWS - WHAT'S INCLUDED

This Aion 4.8 repack includes playable companions, shared quest progress, dungeon repairs and extra tools for trading, appearances and storage.

BUILD YOUR OWN PARTY
Bring your own alternate characters into your party or recruit temporary companions. They fight alongside you, with controls for their combat behavior and equipment. Independent playerbots also quest and travel around the world.

KEEP YOUR ALTS CAUGHT UP
With Party completion sync enabled, your own alts in the party catch up on quests your main has already completed. New quest completions are shared too, including the quest's XP, Kinah and item rewards where provided. Rejoining the party does not pay the same completion again.

DUNGEON AND CAMPAIGN REPAIRS
Recent work covers Theobomos Lab and Adma Stronghold: quest chains, scripted encounters, boss behavior and dungeon exits. Instance entry limits are disabled, so you can return without waiting for the usual entry reset. Level and quest requirements still apply.

TRADING, WARDROBE AND STORAGE
Use the player marketplace to buy and sell items. Wardrobe provides appearance customization, while expanded inventory and warehouse space give you more room for equipment, materials and loot.

GETTING STARTED
Open Additional Functions for companion controls and the extra trading and appearance tools. The repack also includes speech bubbles, a remember-login option and extra graphics settings.
